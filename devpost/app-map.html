Stop 1 · Local Intake & Hashing

src/lib/csvParser.ts & src/lib/dataProfiler.ts

parseAndValidateCsvBuffer() computes the browser Web Crypto SHA-256 digest and enforces 5 MB / 10k-row guardrails. profileDataset() detects missing cells, duplicate IDs (SYN04), -999 sentinels, whitespace/case collisions, and unit conflicts before Step 2 unlocks.

Stop 2 · Deterministic Statistical Kernel

src/lib/scientificEngine.ts & src/python/engine.py

runStatisticalAnalysis() and run_welch_two_group() compute exact two-sided Welch's t-tests (Group B minus Group A sign convention), Satterthwaite degrees of freedom, 95% CIs, and Pearson correlations matching SciPy 1.17.0 to < 1e-9.

Stop 3 · Claim Verifier & Passport

worker/index.ts, src/lib/claimVerifier.ts & src/lib/passport.ts

handleApiRequest() enforces the RAW_ROWS_PROHIBITED (P0) boundary. verifyInterpretationClaims() checks every AI-cited number against result.numbers, and buildEvidencePassport() packages the cryptographic audit trail.

4. A Practice to Reuse

Neuro-Symbolic Contract + Golden Reference Verification

Project Decision & Evidence: Rather than trusting an LLM to compute statistics or summarize raw rows, EvidencePlot AI enforces a three-tier privacy and verification contract (P0 browser-only raw rows, P1 schema metadata for /api/ai/plan, and P2 verified engine numbers for /api/ai/interpret), backed by tests/golden-engine.test.ts (< 1e-9 parity with SciPy 1.17.0 in public/examples/reference_outputs.json) and tests/end-to-end-journey.test.ts.

How to Reuse Next Time: Whenever you build an AI application in a scientific, financial, or engineering domain, separate AI planning/narration from deterministic calculation, validate every AI response against a strict schema + numeric claim verifier (src/lib/claimVerifier.ts), and keep a deterministic fallback mode (Run without AI) so the application remains 100% reliable even when AI is offline or rejects an unverified claim.
